_I never really did daily journalling. But the important stuff, I wrote down._

_I can’t believe it’s already been a year. That makes me feel a bit bittersweet: all this time has passed—and somehow I really did take this lesson to heart. The sun shines brighter because of it. The chill is less grating, and the poems more nuanced._

_I hope this brings you some comfort, as it did I._

**Sep 18, 2025**

I did well! I did as well as I could. Last night I stayed up horribly late (doing english homework) and today I made it my one mission to stay postive. To not let all the little things get to me.

I’m surprised that it worked! I smiled just because. I spoke and pondered things and wrote some ideas down. 

The day wasn’t perfect. It wasn’t much better than any other day of classes. Yet that’s what made it so great. I realized that I have agency over how I feel and act even when faced with in-class writings and last-minute scrapped-together homework assignments and being incredibly sleep-deprived.

I mean it was really bad last night (sorry, _today morning_). I was walking laps every two minutes so that my eyes didn’t seal shut every five words I wrote (I wish that was an exaggeration).

But today I got through it. It almost made me want to pinpoint why. I was staring at the snacks sitting on the counter, thinking. (Incidentally that’s also where I was pacing just the night, sorry _morning_, before. What a difference in mental state!) Was it the prunes I ate yesterday? Or _because_ I slept so little? Maybe a natural kind of hormonal cycle of some sort?

Now I feel I need not have a reason. I consciously made the effort, _I did it_, not the gut-brain axis or whatever. (Well… maybe some intricate biological processes in my body happened to align, altering my psychology just enough to trigger a kind of emergent goodness within my consciousness, or at least the spark of it, which was then fed by my own self-talk, those cycles of thoughts, founded on an innate, almost religious faith I think I might have in myself, and all that was influenced by the incredibly complex landscape of my human experience—in recent times, like the conversations with my brother, but also how I was raised—as well as the daily interactions I carry out, and of course the environment played a role, but I do wonder how independent my contentment today was and could have been of my surroundings…)

Isn’t that great? There’s some beauty in… seeing beauty, I suppose. In drifting through the mind like a balloon, wisping through clouds. 

I just let go. My hand, gripping its plastic string, raised it up like giving a toast before un-contracting. It went up and up and now I can’t even see it but I know it’s having fun in the big blue sky. Maybe a flock of birds has noticed it by now. I love the way certain birds gather, sitting together and then, as if choreographed, suddenly and majestically all fluttering off into the distance, a great rustling that passes by my ear like a wave pushes against the sand of a beach before hemming back.

We live in a cruel and unfair world, and I try to have empathy for, and learn more about, the many people without the same opportunities, treatment, and privileges that I have. I also know that some days _will suck_—that terrible things will happen in my life.

But—_but_—there’s so much good too. Really, there is. There is good everywhere you look, if you try to see it. 

Wallowing doesn’t let me help people. Instead of feeling down 24/7 on behalf of others, I really believe I generally ought to let myself simply enjoy being present. Learning about serious, difficult, gut-wrenching topics helps me be a better person, but there is a time and place for it. Giving myself grace lets me build the mental vivacity, courage, and fortitude needed to solve real problems and to _help people_.

And when I am feeling down from a personal cause—well, that is when it’s important for me to feel emotion vividly. Crumple into a corner, lay on carpet wiping mucus all over my shirt. But I must remember that there is an _other side_.

So… cozy up. Maybe let your lips lift a little. I believe in you, because now I believe in myself.
